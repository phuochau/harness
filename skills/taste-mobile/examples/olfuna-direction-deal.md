## 1. The Object Archive · object-archive

**Affinity clues:** category-fit; mood-fit; material: perfume, bottle, collection (heuristic 15; not a quality score)

- **Design proposition:** Treat owned objects as the primary artifact; the interface feels like opening a private collection, not an inventory dashboard.
- **Composition:** One featured object with quiet metadata; browse by meaningful physical relationships before dense tables.
- **Typography:** Restrained display face for object naming paired with crisp, legible functional text and aligned details.
- **Color roles:** Neutral base drawn from real object materials; one accent reserved for status or an acquisition action.
- **Material / imagery:** Accurate object photography, silhouettes and provenance details; avoid fake luxury texture.
- **Motion:** A captured or selected object transitions into its place in the collection, preserving spatial continuity.
- **Potential signature moment:** Acquisition-to-archive feedback: the new object becomes visibly part of the user's shelf.
- **Risk:** Over-staged imagery can hide sorting, editing and comparison; keep operational controls unambiguous.

## 2. The Editorial Atlas · editorial-atlas

**Affinity clues:** category-fit; mood-fit (heuristic 9; not a quality score)

- **Design proposition:** Give the product's information a clear editorial rhythm, with generous imagery and purposeful captions.
- **Composition:** Asymmetric but aligned type and image fields; sectional rhythm is driven by the user's content hierarchy.
- **Typography:** Distinctive editorial titles combined with unpretentious readable body type; numbers and labels carefully grouped.
- **Color roles:** Ink and paper-like background contrast; accents derived from actual images rather than a generic beige luxury set.
- **Material / imagery:** Photography, excerpts or original content; not decorative stock-paper overlays.
- **Motion:** Measured transitions that preserve reading position and connect article-to-detail or index-to-object.
- **Potential signature moment:** Open an item as if entering its story, while retaining an obvious return to the user's place.
- **Risk:** Magazine composition can slow scanning and edits; keep action affordances overt.

## 3. The Cinematic Story · cinematic-story

**Affinity clues:** category-fit (heuristic 6; not a quality score)

- **Design proposition:** Use transitions, framing and selective imagery to create an emotional narrative without losing navigation.
- **Composition:** A strong scene-level focal image paired with lean task controls and progressive disclosure.
- **Typography:** Expressive display type in key moments; deliberately plain and comfortable reading text for longer passages.
- **Color roles:** Scene-informed palette with controlled contrast and accessible controls on imagery.
- **Material / imagery:** Consistent authentic scenes, portraits or created art; do not suggest unavailable media generation.
- **Motion:** Scene-to-scene continuity with restrained cinematic movement and reduced-motion alternative.
- **Potential signature moment:** A meaningful scene reveal tied to a real event or decision, not an autoplay decorative intro.
- **Risk:** Animation and image loading can sabotage speed and readability on lower-end phones.
