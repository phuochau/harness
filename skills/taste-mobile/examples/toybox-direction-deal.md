## 1. The Tactile Workshop · tactile-workshop

**Affinity clues:** category-fit; mood-fit; material: sticker, toy, drawing (heuristic 15; not a quality score)

- **Design proposition:** Let user-made pieces behave like manipulable physical cutouts, rather than assets trapped in generic cards.
- **Composition:** A clear active canvas with an approachable tray of materials and a small number of large controls.
- **Typography:** Confident friendly letterforms, carefully differentiated display and instruction sizes.
- **Color roles:** Distinct functional colors that mark tools, materials and selection; restrained background so creations stay primary.
- **Material / imagery:** Authentic paper edges, scanned drawings or supplied art; tactile cues should explain dragging/layering.
- **Motion:** Pick-up, placement and snap feedback tied to fingers; an instant non-animated fallback is usable.
- **Potential signature moment:** Transform a real drawing into a movable object with satisfying, understandable placement.
- **Risk:** Playfulness can overfill a small screen; avoid crowded menus and excessive sound/haptics.

## 2. The Kinetic Guide · kinetic-guide

**Affinity clues:** category-fit; mood-fit; material: kid (heuristic 11; not a quality score)

- **Design proposition:** Make next steps tangible through short sequential scenes and direct, encouraging feedback.
- **Composition:** A prominent current step, one next action and consistent progressive reveal of supporting tools.
- **Typography:** Readable instructional text and clear step labels; expressive details never compete with guidance.
- **Color roles:** Distinct action/state colors grounded in an intentional teaching vocabulary.
- **Material / imagery:** Illustrations or objects that demonstrate an actual step, not generic mascots as wallpaper.
- **Motion:** Cause-and-effect movement tied to progress; allow skip and reduced-motion alternatives.
- **Potential signature moment:** Each completed action visibly unlocks or transforms the next real task.
- **Risk:** Too much onboarding can create friction; experienced users need fast paths.

## 3. The Spatial Gallery · spatial-gallery

**Affinity clues:** category-fit; material: sticker (heuristic 8; not a quality score)

- **Design proposition:** Organize visual objects by scale, proximity and meaningful grouping so browsing feels like arranging a place.
- **Composition:** Flexible image-first spatial groups with obvious focus/selection and robust list alternative.
- **Typography:** Understated descriptive labels subordinate to objects; readable details when opened.
- **Color roles:** Background recedes while real objects retain their own colors; single clear control accent.
- **Material / imagery:** User imagery, tasteful shadows only to reinforce spatial layering, uncluttered grouping.
- **Motion:** Objects reflow purposefully on reordering, with minimal motion when reduced motion is enabled.
- **Potential signature moment:** An intuitive arrange-and-rediscover interaction rooted in the objects the user owns.
- **Risk:** Freeform galleries can harm sort/search and accessibility; provide structured alternatives.
