# Skill: Trace

Use at the end of work or when handing off.

## Steps

1. Summarize the run.
2. Link request, brief, tasks, review, and verification.
3. Record files read and changed.
4. Record proof collected, including TDD evidence or exception for production
   implementation.
5. Record proof gaps.
6. Record remaining risk.
7. Record decisions.
8. For experiments, record result and promotion or cleanup decision.
9. Record workflow friction and follow-up work.

## Output

- Trace artifact.
- Decision artifact when needed.
