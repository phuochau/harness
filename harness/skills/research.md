# Skill: Research

Use for a `research` task: learning what options, constraints, or unknowns exist
before committing to a solution. Research gathers knowledge; it does not change
production behavior. When a specific candidate solution needs proof that it
works, use an experiment instead (`docs/delivery/templates/experiment.md`).

## Steps

1. State the question the research must answer.
2. Identify the sources: code, docs, tests, dependencies, providers, or external
   references.
3. Collect evidence and record where each finding came from.
4. Mark remaining unknowns with `NEEDS CLARIFICATION: specific question`.
5. Summarize findings, options, and trade-offs.
6. Recommend the next step: a delivery brief, an experiment, a decision record,
   or a blocker for the human.

## Output

- Research finding note or request update.
- Options and trade-offs.
- Recommendation or blocker.
- A decision record when the finding constrains future work.
