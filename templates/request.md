# Request Template

```yaml
---
id: REQ-000
status: proposed
lane: normal
type: feature
owner: intake
created: YYYY-MM-DD
updated: YYYY-MM-DD
depends_on: []
parallelizable: false
---
```

## Summary

One or two sentences describing the raw request.

## Source

Link or paste the source: human prompt, issue, ticket, bug report, audit request,
customer feedback, or spec.

## Problem

What pain, opportunity, defect, or risk is being addressed?

## Desired Outcome

What should be true when the work succeeds?

## Audience Or Operator

Who benefits from or operates the result?

## Current Behavior

What happens today?

## Target Behavior

What should change?

## User Scenarios

Describe important user or operator journeys in priority order.

- Priority:
- Journey:
- Independent test:

## Edge Cases

- Boundary condition or error case.

## Success Criteria

- Measurable result or observable behavior.

## Non-Goals

What should not be changed?

## Constraints

Product, technical, timing, UX, data, security, or workflow constraints.

## Open Questions

Questions that block or weaken planning. Use `NEEDS CLARIFICATION: specific
question` for ambiguity that should not be guessed.
